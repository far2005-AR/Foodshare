FoodShare is a B2B web application where food providers (restaurants, groceries, caterers,
and community kitchens) create profiles, upload available meals/produce boxes with details
(ingredients, allergens, portion size) and prices or donation flags, and manage customer
orders. End-users (households, students, and community members) can browse items, view
nutrition and pickup/delivery windows, request orders, pay or redeem vouchers, and receive
real-time status updates. The platform acts as a marketplace for multiple organizations to
offer discounted surplus meals and community food boxes across Toronto, supporting both
sales and donation workflows.
